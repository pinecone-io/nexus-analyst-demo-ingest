# Org Announcement: Joining Acme Marketplace Team

**To:** `all-acme-product-and-eng`  
**From:** `victor.okonkwo` (SVP Marketplace)  
**Date:** 2026-04-08  
**Subject:** Welcoming Camille Duarte as Sr PM, Marketplace Seller Experience (Seller Listing + Seller Optimization)

Hi everyone,

I am very pleased to announce that **Camille Duarte** (`assoc_100123`) is joining Acme today as our new **Senior Product Manager for Marketplace Seller Experience**, reporting directly to me. 

As Marketplace has scaled rapidly over the last several quarters—surpassing our prior run-rates and hitting a $3.89B run-rate here in Q2FY27—our seller community has grown right alongside us to roughly 38,000 active sellers across our three sub-verticals: Collectibles, Resold, and Style. 

Up until now, our PM coverage for Marketplace has been heavily vertical-focused. Our sub-vertical product managers—**sanjay.bhatt** (Collectibles), **ines.delgado** (Style), and **noah.kessler** (Resold)—have each done exceptional work owning the buyer-facing View Item Page surfaces, checkout experiences, and category-specific optimizations within their respective domains. Since the decision last spring to formalize our roadmap tracking and clean up our Jira integrations under nadia.esposito’s product-ops initiatives, the team's velocity has been fantastic.

However, as we've reviewed our onboarding funnels, seller feedback loops, and listing workflows, a structural gap has become glaringly obvious: **sellers are a completely separate constituency from buyers**, with entirely distinct onboarding hurdles, tooling requirements, and operational pain points. No single vertical PM currently owns the seller experience end-to-end. 

Camille’s role is purpose-built to address this surface-area blind spot. She will own two critical, cross-vertical domains spanning all three sub-verticals:
1. **Seller Listing:** Simplifying item creation, bulk-upload tooling, catalog management, and compliance touchpoints (including navigating integration workflows like our GradeSure authentication requirements).
2. **Seller Optimization:** Providing merchants with the performance visibility, analytics, and actionable insights they need to understand why listings convert or stall, helping them scale from their first few listings into sustained, high-volume sellers.

Camille comes to us with deep experience scaling multi-tenant merchant platforms and developer ecosystems. She will be sitting on the Marketplace leadership floor (Floor 4, Building B—right next to lucia.ferreira’s Trust & Safety pod). Please stop by, introduce yourselves, and give her a warm Acme welcome!
