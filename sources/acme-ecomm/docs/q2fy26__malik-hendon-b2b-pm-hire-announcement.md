# Acme Internal Org Announcement: Product & Growth

**Date:** June 15, 2025  
**From:** Felix Arroyo, SVP Product & Growth (`felix.arroyo`, `assoc_100010`)  
**To:** All Product, Engineering, Data, and Commercial Teams (`acme-all-org@acme-ecomm.internal`)  
**Subject:** Welcome Malik Hendon — Sr PM, Acme Business (B2B/Wholesale)

Team,

I am thrilled to announce that **Malik Hendon** (`assoc_100160`) is joining Acme today as our Senior Product Manager for **Acme Business (B2B/Wholesale)**, reporting directly to me. 

As many of you who work adjacent to our commercial channels know, Acme Business has operated as one of our 11 'light' verticals without a dedicated product manager since the inception of our digital commerce and marketplace warehouse architecture. While verticals like US Conversion, Marketplace, Care, Speed, and Membership carry deep narrative squads and multi-layered roadmaps, B2B has historically managed its wholesale portal, tier pricing, and invoicing workflows through shared resources and ad-hoc engineering support. 

Malik’s arrival changes that. Bringing him on board makes B2B a *staffed* vertical, though it remains categorized as a light vertical in our taxonomic structure—meaning one dedicated PM does not turn B2B into a deep-dive vertical overnight, but it does give our wholesale buyers, account reps, and large-order clients the focused product leadership they have long needed.

### Malik’s Mandate & Scope

Malik brings over eight years of B2B e-commerce and wholesale platform experience, most recently scaling marketplace and bulk-procurement workflows at enterprise scale. In his new role, Malik’s immediate mandate will center on three core pillars:

1. **Bulk-Order Buyer Experience:** Streamlining the end-to-end checkout, reordering, and quote-request flows for our high-volume B2B and institutional purchasers.
2. **Wholesale-Catalog Modernization:** Overhauling how wholesale inventories, custom SKU sheets, and tier-pricing matrices are managed and displayed across our digital storefronts.
3. **Account-Rep Support Model:** Bridging the tooling gap between our digital self-service features and our internal field sales and account management teams.

He will be partnering closely with our data engineering squads (coordinated through Carlos Figueroa's team, following the recent data analytics promotions and baseline alignment checks established earlier this fiscal year) to ensure our wholesale reporting pipelines are fully integrated into our BigQuery flat dataset structure (`nexus-analyst-demo.acme_ecomm`).
